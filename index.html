Common questions

Before you book a call

How fast can Clearlane start supporting my business? +

Most clients are live within two weeks of their strategy call — the first week is spent mapping your current workflow so nothing gets missed.

Will my customers know they're talking to an outsourced team? +

No. Your assistant communicates under your business name and voice, briefed on your tone, offers, and policies before going live.

Can I keep an eye on how things are going? +

Yes — you get a shared dashboard showing response times, tasks completed, and outstanding items, updated in real time.

What if I only need help with one part of this? +

That's most clients' starting point. We usually begin with a single lane — often intake or follow-ups — and expand once it's proven out.
