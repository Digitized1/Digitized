What we do

Two levers. Pick one, or use both.

Some businesses need a person. Some need a process fixed. Most need a bit of each — Clearlane is built to flex either way.

Service 01

Workflow automation

We map how a lead or client actually moves through your business — then automate every step that doesn't need a human touch.

  • Booking & scheduling that confirms and reminds itself
  • Lead follow-up sequences that never go cold
  • Intake forms routed straight to the right person
  • Reporting that shows up in your inbox, not buried in a dashboard
Service 02

Trained virtual assistants

A dedicated assistant, briefed on your business before day one, handling the parts of ops that still need a human judgment call.

  • Inbox and calendar management
  • Client-facing scheduling and rebooking
  • Order, invoice, and vendor admin
  • Backup coverage so nothing depends on one person's calendar
