About Clearlane

Operations shouldn't be the hardest part of running a business.

We started Clearlane after watching too many good businesses stall — not from lack of demand, but from founders buried in admin they never meant to become experts in.

Why we exist

Built for the messy middle of growth

There's a stage every service business hits: too big for spreadsheets and sticky notes, too small for a full ops department. That's where we work. Clearlane exists to carry founders through that stage without the cost or hassle of a full in-house hire.

How we think

Systems first, people second — always in that order

Before we hand you an assistant, we map the workflow. A person layered onto a broken process just moves the bottleneck. Fixing the lane first means the person we bring in actually makes things faster.

What we believe

Three things we won't compromise on

Your brand, not ours

Every assistant works under your name, your tone, your policies. Clients should never notice a handoff happened.

Visibility over trust-me

You get a live dashboard of what's being handled, not a monthly recap. If something's stuck, you'll see it before we tell you.

Start small, prove it

We'd rather earn the next lane than sell you all four upfront. Most engagements start with a single workflow.

Who it's for

The founders we work best with

Aesthetic & wellness clinics

High booking volume, low tolerance for missed follow-ups.

Coaches & consultants

Client-facing work that shouldn't compete with inbox triage.

Service-based teams

Growing fast enough that "we'll fix it later" stopped working.
