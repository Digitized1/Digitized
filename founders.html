Founders Circle

Built alongside the women running these businesses.

A large share of the founders we support are women juggling growth with two or three other full-time roles. This page is for them.

Why a separate lane

Balancing growth with everything else

Many of the founders in the Circle are scaling a business while also running a household, caregiving, or holding down other work. The admin load lands differently when there's no "just work later tonight" left to give.

What that changes

We plan around real constraints

Onboarding is scheduled around your actual availability, not a standard sales cycle. And your assistant is matched with your working hours and communication style in mind from the start.
